PHOSPHOR COUNT  -  boss respawn timer
====================================

No install needed.

1. Right-click the zip > "Extract All..." > Extract.
   (Don't run it from inside the zip, and keep the folder together:
    PhosphorCount.exe needs the "_internal" folder next to it.)

2. Double-click PhosphorCount.exe.
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info",
   then "Run anyway". (It shows that for any small app that isn't
   signed by a big company.)

Bosses: the row under the title picks the boss (Mushmom, Zombie
Mushmom, Stumpy, Shade, Rotting Mushmom). Click it for a list, or
scroll over it. Each boss has its own timers, and the kill button on
each row is that boss's icon so you always know which list you're in.

Using it:
  +            add a timer (it shows up as Ch. 0 - type the channel, Enter)
  Ch. 0        click it to change the channel number (0-100)
  Boss icon    the boss died: start the respawn countdown
  Copy         copy "Ch. 25 is ready in 12 minutes." to paste in chat
  X            hold for 3 seconds to delete the timer
  BY CH        click to switch between sorting by channel and by time left

When a countdown hits zero the row flashes and keeps counting in red
until you press the boss icon again.

Group sessions: right-click > "Host a group session..." (start clean
or bring your current timers along) and send your friends the
6-letter code; they pick "Join a group session..." and type it. Everyone then shares the same timers, and if two people hit
the kill button at once only the first press counts.

Drag the window anywhere. Right-click for respawn time, opacity and
color palettes.

Your timers and settings are saved in %APPDATA%\PhosphorCount, so they
keep running even if you close the app.

It's only a timer: it never looks at the screen or touches the game.
